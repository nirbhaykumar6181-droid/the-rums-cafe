# Research and business approval register

Do not publish assumed business details. Verify all content with the cafe owner/manager and retain the source or approval date.

| Item | Status | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| Official brand name and logo files | Pending approval | Obtain current approved assets |
| Address and map pin | Pending verification | Confirm exact customer-facing address |
| Phone, email and social links | Pending verification | Confirm active official channels |
| Opening days and hours | Pending verification | Confirm weekly hours and holiday exceptions |
| Menu, descriptions and prices | Pending approval | Obtain current menu and price list |
| Dietary/allergen statements | Pending approval | Obtain cafe-approved wording |
| Reservation policy | Pending approval | Confirm lead time, duration, party-size limits and cancellation rules |
| Seating/table inventory | Pending approval | Confirm table count, capacities and allocation rules |
| Photography and usage rights | Pending approval | Obtain original assets and permission |
| Privacy/contact policy | Pending approval | Confirm data retention and customer contact policy |
| Existing website content | To audit | Compare current live pages before replacing content |

Never infer availability, menu items, prices, hours, or capacity from generic cafe conventions.
